隐私说明

这个网站不收集访客的任何信息：没有统计，没有跟踪，没有 cookie。

在这个域名下登记的小程序，只登录站长自己的账户；除了发给这些账户的公开内容，不读取别人的数据， 不出售、不分享，也不用于广告。它们的权限随时可以在 myaccount.google.com/permissions 撤销。

Privacy

This site collects nothing from its visitors: no analytics, no tracking, no cookies.

The small apps registered under this domain sign in only to the site owner's own accounts. Apart from public content addressed to those accounts, they read no one else's data; nothing is sold, shared or used for ads. Their access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
